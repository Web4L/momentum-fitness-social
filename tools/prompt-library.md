# Prompt Library for Momentum Fitness

Use these prompts with Grok (or other LLMs) to generate high-quality content quickly while staying on brand.

## Core Context to Include in Most Prompts
"You are writing for Momentum Fitness. Our positioning is that we teach the *physics* behind effective movement. We are educational, clear, confident, and encouraging. Primary goal right now is driving qualified leads into Physics 101 classes. Use simple language to explain movement concepts. Avoid generic fitness bro language."

## Reel / Short Video Hooks
**Prompt:**
"Generate 8 strong Reel hooks for Momentum Fitness focused on Physics 101. Each hook should create curiosity or challenge a common belief. Keep them under 8 seconds when spoken. Format as numbered list."

## Educational Carousel Ideas
**Prompt:**
"Create 5 carousel post ideas for Instagram that explain a movement principle from Momentum's MOVE framework. Each idea should have a strong hook in slide 1 and clear educational value. Focus on Physics 101 positioning."

## Physics 101 Specific Content
**Prompt:**
"Write 6 Instagram content ideas (mix of Reels and Carousels) designed to drive leads into Physics 101 classes. Include suggested hook, format, and CTA for each."

## Ad Copy Variations
**Prompt:**
"Generate 5 variations of ad copy for a Physics 101 intro offer. Tone should be educational and benefit-focused rather than hype. Target people who are tired of generic gym programs."

## Caption Writing
**Prompt:**
"Write a strong Instagram caption for a Physics 101 Reel. Hook in the first 2 lines. Include educational value, subtle social proof, and a clear but soft CTA toward Physics 101. Keep it conversational."

## Content Repurposing
**Prompt:**
"Take this longer piece of content about [topic] and turn it into 3 different Instagram formats: 1 Reel concept, 1 carousel outline, and 1 Story series idea."

## Client Story / Testimonial Framing
**Prompt:**
"Turn this client result into 3 Instagram post concepts. Focus on what they learned in Physics 101 and how it changed their training. Make it relatable for someone considering starting."

## Weekly Content Planning
**Prompt:**
"Create a balanced 7-day Instagram content plan for Momentum Fitness. Prioritize Physics 101 positioning and lead generation. Mix educational, proof, and culture content. Suggest formats for each day."
